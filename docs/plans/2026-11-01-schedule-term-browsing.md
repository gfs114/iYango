# School term browsing — approved design

Tapping the Schedule title or semester/date subtitle opens a unified bottom sheet, with the school term selector above the week grid. Selecting a term opens an independent read-only projection, never calls sync prepare/commit or changes global current timetable. Today, reminders, calendar and widgets keep observing existing data.

A dedicated private persistent cache isolates school/account identity and term. Only options received from the school (including cached options) are selectable. The school snapshot is the browse source. Only known semester date metadata may be reused from a local table with the exact same connection and term; local courses and adjustments remain in the independent current timetable. Browsing refresh replaces only the selected cache after a successful complete response, and failures retain the previous result. Cancellation plus request identity fences prevent late account/term responses from replacing current UI.

Unknown semester dates use an internal Monday anchor only for week resolution. UI hides dates, current-day/current-week indicators and exams and says 日期待设置. History starts at week 1; each term remembers its selected week during browsing. Return-to-current restores the ordinary Schedule projection. Browsing permits details and week navigation while rejecting all mutations at UI and ViewModel boundaries.

Pull-to-refresh is an explicit return to the existing current timetable and today's teaching week, with school synchronization continuing in the background. A single-grid 300 ms fade and small vertical transition finishes before the local-success notice; the exact current-week pager position must also be acknowledged. The pull indicator does not wait for school synchronization. Missing local data, invalid teaching weeks and failed local preparation never report success. Leaving the tab or choosing another week cancels the pending local notice. The chooser's retry remains scoped to the selected school term, and background school success does not produce a duplicate success toast.

Validation: JVM coroutine/controller tests, projection tests, Compose header/detail tests, focused feature/data tests and offline debug build. No school network requests, device use, installation, commits or pushes.
